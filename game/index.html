SOLMAR BAY

A SOLMAR BAY STORY — THE SOLMAR JOB
Mara Voss is back in the city that made her — sun, salt air and bad decisions. Dante Reyes has a job lined up: three scores, one payday, and the whole Solmar Bay PD in the way. Steal a car. Run the Bayfront. Take the bank. Don't get caught.
WASD / Arrows — move & drive  ·  Shift — run  ·  Mouse — camera
E — enter / exit car  ·  Click — shoot  ·  Space — jump
P / Esc — pause  ·  M — mute  ·  R — get back up after WASTED / BUSTED
An original game. Solmar Bay, Mara Voss, Dante Reyes and every vehicle, street and song are invented for this game — not affiliated with Rockstar Games.
